rclone OAuth client

This page exists to provide the home page and privacy policy that Google requires before a personal OAuth client can be moved out of testing status.

The client is used with rclone, an open source command line program, to copy files between a personal computer and the Google Drive account of the same person. It is not a product, it is not distributed to anyone, and it has no users other than its owner.
